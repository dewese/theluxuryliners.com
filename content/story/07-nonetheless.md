The journal stopped in March 2004, but the band kept playing. Gary Ishee joined them for several shows in 2004 and 2005. In 2005 “Promise Ring”, a song on no album, came out on *Between Goodlettsville And Murfreesboro*, a compilation from Sound Asleep Records.

*Nonetheless* came out in October 2006 on Litterbug Records, and it is the trio’s record: David wrote the songs and sang and played guitar, Larry played bass and keyboards, Scott played drums and percussion, and both of them sang too. Guests filled out the sound, with Ryan Gilbert on keyboards and vocals, Gary Ishee on guitar, Erika Proegler on violin and Brent Milligan on cello. Chris Henning mixed and mastered it at The Den in Ferndale, California.

Doli Stepniewski played the CD release show. At the end of the year Absolute Power Pop put *Nonetheless* at number 100 in its top 100 releases of 2006, and Carligula put it at number 3 in its top five Nashville releases.

In 2007 the band played shows with Grant Johnson and Jeremy Stephens.
