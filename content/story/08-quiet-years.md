In 2008 David released his first solo album, *Make The Best Of It*. At its CD-release show, at Opening Bell Coffee in downtown Dallas, Chad joined him on stage for a few songs: Chad on banjo, David on acoustic guitar.

The Luxury Liners slowly stopped touring and releasing music in the late 2000s, and David left Nashville in 2009. But none of the four ever said the band was over.
