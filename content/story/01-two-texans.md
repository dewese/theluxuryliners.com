In the winter and spring of 1997, David Dewese and his college friend Chad Edgington traded emails about starting a band. Chad was finishing college, and David was in Texas. Over spring break 1997 they got together to record a pile of songs on a 4-track, and in those sessions they came up with the name: The Luxury Liners, after the Gram Parsons song “Luxury Liner”.

Both of them played guitar and bass, and both of them sang. In May 1997, after Chad’s graduation, they moved to Nashville to make it a working band.

Nashville became the band’s home town: the one city where all four forever members would live while they chased the band.
